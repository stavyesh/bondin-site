it started with a boy named Buli

our story

My dog-child, my best friend, my inspiration.

Buli brought joy, laughter, and purpose to every single day. Let's be honest though, those cute ears don't come with instructions. Like a lot of dog parents, I wanted to give him the very best. Instead, I found myself scrolling for hours, standing in aisles, squinting at labels while he (im)patiently waited.

What I actually wanted was more time with him. More play, more laughter, more little moments that turn into memories. So we built Bondin to do the searching and the curating, every month, so you can spend less time scrolling and more time on the couch, on the trail, or wherever your pup happens to be dragging you.
